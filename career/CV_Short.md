## Single PDF RAG Assistant

Getamesay Zegeye | Independent AI portfolio project | October 2026

Python, Streamlit, Sentence Transformers, MiniLM, FAISS, Google Gen AI SDK

- Built a Streamlit PDF question-answering application using local MiniLM embeddings, persistent FAISS search and Gemini Flash-Lite responses with page citations.
- Combined semantic and keyword retrieval with same-page neighbor expansion to recover evidence missed by vector search.
- Improved a 12-question development/regression evaluation from 11/12 reviewed passes to 12/12 developer-reported passes; results are limited to one research PDF.
